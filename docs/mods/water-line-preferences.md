# Water-line area preferences

Area reactions come from the partner’s `favored_region` and each loaded `.MAP`’s
`regionA`/`regionB` lists. The engine checks A then B at each of four positions;
the first matching entry determines pleased (1), displeased (2), or neutral (0).
The lists below use the built extended-story maps, including custom screens.
Screens without a `.MAP` (50 and 61) and empty table slots have no area setup.

## Before

All screens omitted from a list are neutral for that species.

## After: region 9 for all three species

No existing region combines the requested cold/water likes and lava dislikes.
Region 1 likes Freezeland but is neutral in Ice Sanctuary and at Dragon Eye Lake;
region 8 likes the lake but dislikes Freezeland. A new profile, 9, uses the engine’s
existing byte comparisons and spare entries in the map lists. No engine patch,
allocation, save layout change, or change to another species’ reactions is needed.

The cold screens include Ancient Glacial Region and Leomon Ancestor’s Cave.
Water screens include the lake, its bridge and terrace, Coela Point, the mangroves,
Underground Pond, Beetle Land’s landing screen, the Secret Beach Cave and Drowned
Shore. The other Beetle Land forest screens stay neutral. Every Lava Cave variant
is disliked; the other Drill Tunnel rooms stay neutral except Underground Pond.

All other screens are neutral for all three. `content/map-regions.json` is the
profile source. Native map generation and the story compiler apply it after map
edits; localized sprite-map copies and the two custom water-screen recipes also
apply it. Repeated application is

another region. Map, story and localized-sign cache inputs include the profile data.
