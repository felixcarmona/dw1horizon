Digimon World: Horizon is a fan-made mod for the original PlayStation Digimon World, with additional Digimon, gameplay options, and the story expansion *What the island forgot*. This first release is the “everything” build; choose your options in the game.

**Bring your own four discs:** a supported Digimon World release, Digital Card Battle, Digimon World 2, and European Digimon World 2003 or Japanese Digimon World 3. USA World 3 and the first Japanese pressing of World 1 are unsupported. Use clean dumps; the patcher checks supported versions.

- **Web:** visit https://dw1horizon.com/, add your four disc images, build, and save the patched `.bin` and `.cue` together. Use a desktop browser with workers and local file storage support; allow several minutes and several GB of free space and memory.
- **CLI:** extract the package, keep its files together, and run Python 3.10+ (on Windows, use `py` instead of `python3`):

```sh
python3 dwpatch.py apply "World 1.cue" --source-disc "dcb=Card Battle.bin" --source-disc "dw2=World 2.bin" --source-disc "dw2003=World 2003.bin"
```

Open the resulting `.cue` in your emulator with its `.bin` beside it. Your source images stay unchanged. The package README lists supported dumps and troubleshooting steps.

English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese and Catalan are selectable at first launch and in OPTIONS. Translations are beta drafts with partial editorial review; Japanese is our adaptation from Spanish, rather than the original Japanese script. Keep a backup of your memory card before switching builds. This early release has limited play coverage; report problems with your patcher log, disc region and language. Original PlayStation 2 MB memory limits remain the build target.
