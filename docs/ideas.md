# Ideas backlog

Ideas from players and from development that have been noted but **not selected for development**. Being listed here is not a
promise: an idea may be done later, changed, or never done. Ideas that were built, or decided against, are moved out of this list
(see [decisions.md](decisions.md) for deliberate changes once it exists).

## Open questions (being discussed)

- **How long a Rookie waits before evolving into a Champion:** 72 hours (the original game, current) or 96 hours (one more day with the
  Rookie). A community poll is open in the Discord's polls channel; its result will be weighed, but it isn't binding.
- **A bigger rebalance of ground item odds.** 0.0.6 raises rare finds to at least 8 % per visit (1 % stat foods to 3 %); a player is
  preparing a full per-item proposal, which needs its own audit.
- **Randomizer:** which parts to randomize and whether it fits the patcher.

## Raising and evolution

- **Evolve early on purpose.** An NPC, item or menu option to evolve a partner before its timer once its requirements are met (a cost or
  a reduced lifespan were suggested). Came up in the Rookie wait poll.
- **Delay an evolution.** An item or NPC that keeps a partner in its current stage longer.
- **Extended story with only the original partners.** A setting that keeps the new maps and recruits but limits partners to the original
  game's Digimon until the new ones are polished.
- **Unique finishers for the new Digimon.** Many imported Digimon reuse another Digimon's finisher (for example a shuriken or a rocket
  that doesn't fit them).
- **DarkSuperStarmon.** An alternative Starmon evolution with its own Battle/Machine/Fire identity, once one of its Megas is in the game.
- **Beelzemon and its Blast Mode.** The normal Beelzemon as a partner, with a sidequest that unlocks Blast Mode.
- **WarGreymon from Digimon World 2's model** instead of the current one.
- **Grey Lord's Mansion by type.** Revisit which Digimon may enter where (a player expected a Virus partner to get in).
- **Ophanimon.** Missing because none of the four supported discs has a model of it; it would have to be built from another model.

## Items, shops and the economy

- **Item icons in the Item Bank** list, like in the inventory.
- **Portable Potty used directly.** Giving it to the partner makes it use it instead of eating it.
- **A deterministic daily seed for ground items.** The item a spot offers is fixed for the day and entering only changes whether it is
  there. Needs a full rebalance of every spot.
- **More vending machines** around the island as surprises.

## Interface

- **Result stats finish on their own.** After training or battle the stat counters could animate quickly and close by themselves.
- **Keep the key as a key item** instead of an ordinary item.

## Patcher

- **A faster way to build on slow devices.** Phones can take hours to build the disc; a lighter or prebuilt-delta path was suggested.

## Languages

- **Simplified Chinese** (in progress on a branch; 8 MB only, because its font doesn't fit in 2 MB).

## Community
