# Ideas backlog

Ideas from players and from development that are noted but **not selected for development**. Being listed here is not a promise: an
idea may be done later, changed, or never done. Each one was checked for viability before being listed. Ideas that are built, or decided
against, leave this list.

**Kinds:**
- 🎭 **Personal taste:** no right answer; it depends on how each player likes to play. Best as an option or after a vote.
- ⚖️ **Balance:** changes difficulty or the economy; needs numbers and an audit.
- 🛠️ **Quality of life:** a clear improvement with little debate.
- ✨ **Polish:** makes what already exists look or work better.
- 🆕 **New content or mode:** adds something that doesn't exist yet.
