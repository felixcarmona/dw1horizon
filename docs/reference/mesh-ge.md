# Mesh investigation: 405, 407, 471, 473, 477

Three cause repairs ship in this worktree. Nyokimon passes the unchanged native
envelope; the other four still fail. The retained floors have explicit bounds:
they do **not** prove that further reshaping or motion redesign is impossible.
No model receives unrestricted visual clearance.

The audit (regenerated by `python3 -m tools.models.mesh_ge_audit`; output kept under `build/`) retains detector results, per-slot coverage,
residual pairs, preservation, storage, render measurements and split-budget
inputs. Before/after compressed CSVs in that directory retain **every located
crossing per clip/frame**, including shallow contacts, adjacent bones, object
numbers, triangle-pair counts and symmetric depths. Coverage is 214 occupied
slots / 8,411 decoded ticks at each stage. Frame numbers are zero-based.

## Cause repairs

**405:** the neck tuck drives head bone 4 into torso bone 2. Lift neck bone 3
by a 15-degree pitch correction weighted by sin² across slots 35, 40 and 41.
The correction and its continuous derivative are zero at either endpoint;
integer poses retain both endpoints exactly. The original acting, translations,
root travel and events remain. The pair clears throughout all three clips:

| Slot | Triangle pairs / depth at deepest record before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 35 | 3 / 91.291 | 0 / 0 |
| 40 | 23 / 134.944 | 0 / 0 |
| 41 | 25 / 133.417 | 0 / 0 |

Counts in this table are at the deepest crossing record, not necessarily the
largest pair count elsewhere in the clip; the CSV retains both measurements.
There is no new crossing bone pair in these clips. The adjoining torso/neck
contact deepens by 2.006 units in slot 40 and 1.983 in slot 41. Slot 35 improves.
Floor excursions and seams are exact; maximum vertex steps do not increase.
The existing large step in slot 35 remains. Other head tucks remain, including
slot 53 frame 28 at 130.135 units. Broader pulse trials are recorded rather than
shipping unreviewed changes to every attack.

**407:** open rear hip 15's yaw by +8 degrees and rear hip 24's by −4 degrees
in walk slots 2 and 3. Initial rotations and absolute keys receive the offset;
incremental motion retains its original easing and swing. Walk clears bones
14/16, 14/17 and 20/26; condition walk clears 14/17 and 20/26. Their respective
deepest records were 62.483, 36.960, 61.837, 59.557 and 61.572 units. Neither
clip introduces a crossing bone pair or increases an existing pair's maximum
depth by more than one unit. The old run correction remains.

Whole-model floor sink becomes .004482 and .014775 idle heights, below the
existing .05-height QA tolerance. Both loop seams remain zero. Maximum vertex
steps remain .240638 heights in walk and change .190226 → .190690 in condition
walk. The already removed zero-surface mouth quad frees 40 packet bytes; with
eight previously unused bytes, it funds one further 28-byte bent-quad split.
That split keeps both GPU triangles and every corner attribute.

**473:** the terminal stem protrudes through the bases of both leaves. Retract
its eight terminal-ring vertices by two local Y units and its cap by three.
The cap then meets the existing leaf centre at (−12, −87, −2), retaining a
connected graft. Transform affected normals with the inverse deformation.
All leaf geometry, animation and textures remain. The tie rate falls 75.3%.
This is an attachment repair, not an arbitrary bias of overlapping leaf faces.

**471 and 477:** retain their incoming geometry and animation. Pichimon's
remaining failures are distributed bent quads; its feeler/body and small-piece
contacts remain. Yokomon's six stamens and central curl overlap within object
1, bone 3, independently of its moving petal contacts. The
[experiment archive](mesh-ge/experiments.json.gz) includes stamen spreads,
tube fits, curl offsets, alternate split priorities and pose trials. The best
recorded whole-piece spread/curl trial still reverses at .072885, above .072223;
these large root shifts threaten the connection to the receptacle. Thinning
the hooks and smooth anchored bends also fail. None is shipped simply to
change the aggregate score.

## Bounded floors

The fractional packet-budget relaxation in `mesh_ge_audit` permits fractional
splits and ignores all resident costs. It therefore overestimates how much
bent risk any subset of existing quad splits can remove. It includes lossless
flat-shading savings, the existing toe fit, and the zero-surface mouth removal.
Even this optimistic bound cannot reach the native bent-risk limit:

| ID | Lower bound on residual bent risk / native limit |
| --- | ---: |
| 405 | 2.548× |
| 407 | 16.730× |
| 471 | 1.713× |

This proof fixes the current rest-height normalization and oriented surface
triangles. It excludes new triangle joins, reshaping, approximate shading,
additional deletion, and a larger packet allowance. The test suite compares the
relaxation against exact small knapsack enumerations to check the bound's direction.

The [translation certificates](mesh-ge/certificates.json.gz) independently
verify every leaf of a complete octant subdivision using strict determinant
signs at all eight corners. Each part may translate independently within the
listed world-axis cube and at least one crossing remains:

| ID / bones | Slot / frame | Independent bound per part, each axis | Clear local rotation trials / 250 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 405 / 2,5 | 53 / 28 | ±4 units | 0 |
| 407 / 11,14 | 3 / 24 | ±2 units | 35 |
| 471 / 2,5 | 8 / 50 | ±2 units | 0 |
| 477 / 3,4 | 43 / 57 | ±4 units | 0 |

Rotation trials are finite, not a continuous proof. In particular, 407 can
clear the witness in a single pose; that does not establish a safe clip-wide
repair. A separate [same-bone certificate](mesh-ge/477-self-floor.json.gz)
retains 21 strict stamen/curl component crossings in local space. A common
invertible affine bone transform preserves those intersections, so joint
animation alone cannot clear them. Local reshaping or reassignment can evade
this floor. No unrestricted repair floor is claimed for any species.
