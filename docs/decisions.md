# Gameplay and design decisions

Did something change on purpose? Search this page for the item, Digimon, place or option.
Each entry gives the change, its reason and a source for the details. Dates are decision
or merge dates, not release dates; **by 2026-10-30** means present in the initial public
source snapshot, with no more precise date established here. This inventory covers that
snapshot and merged work through **2026-11-05, PR #191**. A merge can precede the release
that includes it; check the [release changelog](../web/content/changelog.json) for availability.

Entries describe current rules unless marked **earlier**, **superseded** or **not shipped**.
Grouped model repairs share an entry where they implement the same design. Data and
implementation take precedence over older prose: in particular, the current evolution
timers and roster totals below supersede conflicting historical paragraphs in the balance, City and partner guides.
Routine build fixes and defects that restore the documented behavior are not new balance rules.
Keep this page updated when a deliberate change is merged, including reversals and their reasons.

## Economy & items

- **2026-11-06 — Legacy care-badge placement:** retain the exact v0.0.6 lower-right anchor, offsets and standalone rule. Only the button glyph and distance/facing independence differ from the old care prompt; eligibility, condition bubbles, scale, animation and foreground order remain unchanged. Compare projected GPU quad offsets against the original badge ([design](../DESIGN.md)).
- **2026-11-05 — Square quick care:** physical Square opens the care list from any distance in normal field control, with icon 1 (the native round gray cap and pink Square). The care badge keeps its position, projected size and animation. Confirm is reserved for talking/interacting in the field, while menu/battle Square actions keep their existing ownership. Tutorial and Jijimon care hints explain it in all nine languages ([care controls](release/README-players.md#quick-care-controls), [design](../DESIGN.md)).
- **2026-10-24 — Economy rule:** repeatable purchases, trades, card conversions and expected random payouts must never turn bits into more bits; earning money should require play rather than a conversion loop ([balance: economy rule](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [audit](../tools/economy_audit.py)).
- **2026-11-05 — Useful mushroom exchange:** group 1 exchanges Happy Mushroom for Deluxe Mushroom once daily; Mammothmon already sells the former Ice Mushroom reward. Deluxe grants +100 HP/MP and +10 to each other stat (about 240 points spread versus about 900 from a training day). Happy Mushroom is scarce: lake vending sells one/day for 1500 and ground rolls are rare. Buying the input and selling Deluxe nets at most 1000/day, below a 1500-bit Birdramon trip; trading an owned input gains 2000 over its 500-bit sale value. Prices stay unchanged. The eight daily rows now gain 2200 over selling all inputs, or at most 3800 when choosing positive sale-value rows; gross rewards sell for 8050. This supersedes the earlier trade totals ([balance and gain baselines](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **2026-11-05 — Separate Dolphmon evolution item and measured trade gains:** North Star stays Whamon's arena reward and all eight descriptions are restored. Dolph Fin reuses unused item 124 and the existing item sprite with a blue palette; the fourth existing custom evolution rule makes a Rookie evolve to Dolphmon (238), value 5000. Small Recovery → Dolph Fin is once per save under permanent flag 13; the other eight exchanges remain daily. Old ninth-row completion remains spent. Tapirmon-only root pricing is skipped because generic shops expose global prices only; Vegiemon is unchanged. Daily incremental gain is 200 for all eight rows or a ceiling of 1800 by selecting positive sale-value rows (300 after Birdramon), rather than the rewards' 6050 gross. Buying only profitable purchasable inputs yields 999 normally or 1109 with baby discounts, before travel; Digiseabass is not purchasable. This supersedes the earlier North Star retarget and gross-ceiling language ([trade limits and both gain baselines](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [evolution items](reference/partner.md#items-handleevoitems-itemc2179)).
- **2026-11-05 — Mojyamon bounded gains and North Star (earlier, superseded above):** eight rows are daily, with exact reviewed sale-value exceptions; Small Recovery → North Star is once per save using permanent completion flag 13, never daily. Group 1 uses Digisnapper → Digipine; group 2 uses Supercarrot → Large MP, Hawk Radish → Large Recovery and Blue Apple → Double Floppy; group 3 uses Digimushroom → Auto Pilot and Giant Meat → DigiTrout before its once-only North Star row. North Star now targets Dolphmon (238), including its eligibility reader and all eight descriptions. Vegiemon stays at 500 per root. The daily rewards total 6050 bits with free inputs; buying every sold input plus free-fishing the unsold Digiseabass nets 2099 normally or 2309 with baby discounts, before 1500-bit Birdramon travel. The one-time North Star reward is excluded from those daily totals ([full limits, prices and ceilings](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)). This adds bounded exceptions to the economy rule and supersedes the earlier daily-exchange mapping.
- **2026-11-05 — Mojyamon daily exchanges (earlier, superseded above):** each of nine rows is available once per game day, save-persistent, with an amount cap of one and refusal text in eight languages. Group 3 alone recruits after all nine historical exchanges; older recruited saves remain intact. Supercarrot replaces the proposed Digimushroom input for Auto Pilot because 50 → 150 would violate the sale-value rule. North Star still targets Whamon and no Dolphmon evolution item exists, so no gift is added. Tapirmon replaces Digipine with Hawk Radish at normal shop prices; it feeds no exchange ([current trade table](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **2026-10-24 — Mojyamon trades (earlier, superseded above):** Digimushroom → Small Recovery, Supercarrot → Offense Disk, Happy Mushroom → Medicine, Giant Meat → Auto Pilot, Black Trout → Rest Pillow, HiSpeed Disk → Defense Disk, Omnipotent → Large MP, Medium Recovery → MP Floppy and Digianchovy → Meat replace the disc's output list; every trade must pay no more than its input's best sale/conversion value ([balance: before/after trade list](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [shop table](../content/tables/shops.json)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Card merit prices:** Sup. Recovery costs 60 merit instead of 20 and Sup. Restore 250 instead of 100; the old cash-out made a 500-bit card pack worth about 1090 bits ([balance: economy rule](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Card machine odds:** rarity 2 falls from 7% to 5% and rarity 4 rises from 70% to 72%; card resale and NPC conversion chains previously returned more than the 100-bit price ([economy table](../content/tables/economy.json), [balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Tekkamon drop:** S. Defense Disk falls from a guaranteed drop to 15%; unrestricted Back Dimension fights paid excessive repeatable cash ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [species record](../content/species/90-tekkamon.json)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Battle money:** wins pay 150% of enemies' listed bits; ordinary battles should fund upkeep and make saving worthwhile ([economy table](../content/tables/economy.json), [balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Wild Psychemon/Gururumon rewards:** listed bits rise from 200/200 to 300/400; their rewards should match original enemies with similar offense ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Meat and starting supplies:** normal Meat value falls from 50 to 30 bits and a real new game receives 20 Meat and 1000 bits; the farm alone did not cover the first training days ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [opening script](../story/base/zones/file-city/s193-238/script.py)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Farm harvest:** the five-item harvest occurs one day in four instead of one in ten, with three items otherwise; the daily farm should provide a useful share of food ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Daily ground cap:** a ground spot gives at most one successful pickup until midnight, retained through save/continue; repeated map entries previously generated unlimited free items ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [patch index, former 0230/0265](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Batch transactions:** Mojyamon trades, card packs and the card machine ask how many and show the resulting cards together; repeated exchanges should not require one dialogue per item ([items and menus](reference/items-and-menus.md#item-keeper-mojyamon-birdramon-jukebox), [patch index, former 0223–0228/0237](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **2026-10-31 — Monochromon shift:** recruitment needs 2560 profit instead of 3072, the top halfway comment starts at 1280 instead of 1536, and a profit/customer plate shows progress; recruitment was too unlikely even with sensible pricing, while payout tiers, customers and odds stay the same ([balance: Monochromon](mods/balance.md#63-monochromon-recruitment); PR #16).
- **2026-11-01 — Harvest reminder:** a meat bubble appears above the farmer while a harvest waits; players should see that food is ready without another conversation ([patch index, former 0315–0316](../mods/patches/index.md); PRs #71, #72).
- **2026-11-02 — Restaurants:** meals cost 400–1500 bits instead of 300–9000 and give 8–42 stat points plus 35 energy instead of 1–5 points; buying a meal should be useful beside chips and training ([balance: care and reward pass](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Digitamamon meals:** three all-stat outcomes use 30/30/40% instead of equal thirds; a late recruit's restaurant should give a satisfying reward ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Drimogemon hunts:** the 3-day/1000-bit and 10-day/5000-bit plans become 1-day/2000-bit and 3-day/5000-bit plans with new reward tables and a 5% extra-item roll with Gabumon; hunts should be timely without becoming daily resale profit ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Merit shop quantities:** cards can be traded and merit items bought in chosen amounts, with an open results list; bulk transactions should take fewer repeated inputs ([items and menus](reference/items-and-menus.md#cards-and-the-merit-shop); PR #86).
- **2026-11-03 — Ground pickup positions:** new-map items move onto generously reachable floor, later with four clear rows behind them; visible mushrooms should be collectible and should not draw on walls ([pickup registry](../content/ground-items.json), [map rules](mods/new-maps.md); PRs #99, #159, #177).
- **2026-11-03 — New-map mushroom count:** the 34 maps borrowing Native Forest's seven spawns keep only three daily spots: Deluxe Mushroom at 2% and Digimushrooms at 25% and 15%; seven reachable spots per map produced about 240 free daily items, while Native Forest keeps seven ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [pickup registry](../content/ground-items.json); PR #100).
- **2026-11-03 — Mansion Key:** opening the last of seven locked doors consumes the key, with an explanation; it no longer occupies a useless bag slot, and older saves clear it on entering the hall once all doors are open ([mansion hall script](../story/base/zones/grey-lords-mansion/s052-53/script.py); PR #107).
- **2026-11-03 — Tuskmon's shop:** after Monochromon joins, Tuskmon runs the original shop and offers one daily bargain at 2500 bits, haggled to 2150 then 1800 with 60% then 40% chances; the building keeps a use, and the minimum exceeds resale value ([shop implementation](../story/mods/what-the-island-forgot/arcs/canyon/monochrome-shop.py); PR #112).
- **2026-11-04 — Root Clearing drops:** its two Goblimon now drop Meat at 25%, superseding the brief Blue Apple change; the clearing should supply food ([map content](../content/maps), [balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule); PRs #148, #164).
- **2026-11-04 — Secret Item Shop:** Devimon sells only DVChipA/D/E again; plain chips alongside them undermined their role, so plain chips remain at 800 merit and in chests ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule), [shop script](../story/mods/what-the-island-forgot/city/secret-item-shop.py); PR #164).
- **2026-11-04 — Dragon Eye Lake vending:** unlimited Digimushrooms cost 51, Happy Mushrooms 1500 and Ice Mushrooms 1000 with one of each rare mushroom daily; useful stock must respect Mojyamon conversion values and save-persistent daily limits ([balance: vending machines](mods/balance.md#63-vending-machines); PR #175).
- **2026-11-04 — Ancient Glacial Region vending:** the former 200-bit surprise machine sells Moldy Meat 26, Meat 20, Giant Meat 300 and Sirloin 1000 without daily limits; the machine gets a food role and Moldy Meat stays above its 25-bit sale value ([balance: vending machines](mods/balance.md#63-vending-machines); PR #175, superseding #146's old random-stock fix).
- **2026-11-04 — Gear Savanna vending:** Small Recovery costs 118, Medium Recovery 318, MP Floppy 218 and Medium MP 500, with the original 9% Double Floppy bonus and separate MP Station; prices include the bonus's resale value so expected returns remain below cost ([balance: vending machines](mods/balance.md#63-vending-machines); PR #175).
- **2026-11-04 — Tropical Jungle vending:** its MP Station becomes a 1000-bit prize draw, with eight recovery/utility outcomes at 10% each and Restore/three disks at 5% each; every roll gives a prize, payment precedes the roll and a full bag loses the prize, preventing free selective rerolls ([balance: full prize list and odds](mods/balance.md#63-vending-machines); PR #175).

- **2026-11-05 — Amida Forest odds:** Orange Banana/Happy Mushroom/Auto Pilot use 8/12/20%, Omni Disk/Medium MP use 8/25%, taking probability from empty rolls; useful finds become more visible while preserving rarity order, companion outcomes and the stop after Centarumon joins ([balance](mods/balance.md#61-the-economy-rule); PR #188).

## Evolution & care

- **2026-11-05 — Balanced Baby II chart:** with NEW DIGIMON on, all eight In-Trainings have six ordinary Rookie targets. Canon Tsunomon → Gabumon joins the existing Yokomon → Biyomon, Motimon → Tentomon, Bukamon → Gomamon and Nyaromon → Salamon anchors; all original-disc routes and Kunemon's special stay. A one-entry canon-link allow-list preserves Tsunomon's Air/Ice identity. Gabumon's Tsunomon route uses weight 26 through a contextual weight row, while its original Koromon route keeps weight 15; moved Rookies receive practical requirements without replacing original normal-weight outcomes. The chart needs no extra page for any baby ([implemented design](proposals/baby-rookie-chart.md), [partner rules](reference/partner.md#evolution-rules-for-the-added-species)).

- **2026-11-06 — Reachable evolution alternatives:** Dokugumon asks for offense/brains instead of offense/speed, separating its picker average from Stingmon on Wormmon's list. Tylomon asks for defense/speed instead of HP/defense, separating it from Ikkakumon on Gomamon's list. Earlier siblings won both identical-average ties even outside their weight bands because their parent bonuses supplied the missing point. WaruSeadramon's bonus now comes from 50 battles instead of a free Seadramon parent point: Megadramon's canonical 30-battle state keeps Megadramon. Simply deleting the parent point also changed a qualifying loser's carried average and let Whamon take some existing Megadramon states; the battle bonus preserves those sampled winners ([requirements](../content/species/112-waruseadramon.json), [picker regressions](../tools/test_evo_tree_guard.py)).
- **2026-10-15 — Added Rookie routes:** In-Training partners gain extra Rookie targets with distinct requirements, including overfed/underfed weight bands, while canonical original states still choose their original child; new partners should extend the tree without replacing ordinary original raising ([partner: added species](reference/partner.md#evolution-rules-for-the-added-species), [tree data](../content/tables/evolution-tree.json)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Care menu (earlier button binding):** confirm in front of the partner offers relevant food, cures, toilet, sleep and fair scolding, with urgent needs first, favorite food marked and an interaction badge; care should take fewer menu steps without changing item refusals ([items and menus: using an item](reference/items-and-menus.md#using-an-item)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Condition bubbles:** active needs use a fixed row of equal bubbles, superseding the earlier rotating carousel; simultaneous conditions should stay legible ([patch index, former 0069–0077/0155–0156](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Pause vitals:** the triangle menu shows current/max partner HP and MP; opening the menu should give immediate access to combat condition ([items and menus](reference/items-and-menus.md#triangle-menu-and-digimon-screens), [patch index, former 0221–0222](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Repeated evolutions:** an already-seen evolution can be skipped with Start and an on-screen hint; repeated raising should involve less waiting ([patch index, former 0185/0188/0200/0204](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Gym blocks:** R2 selects a block of up to five training sessions, stopping for relevant care, learning or evolution; repeated training should need fewer inputs while still applying each session's costs ([minigames: training](reference/minigames.md#training-trn-and-trn2), [patch index, former 0186/0189/0196](../mods/patches/index.md)).
- **2026-10-31 — Seven-question starter quiz:** all seven three-answer questions determine one of 35 Rookies with no random draw, resolving ties by the latest supporting answer then axis order; a new partner should follow the player's answers, and NEW DIGIMON off uses the nine original Rookies ([balance: quiz](mods/balance.md#jijimons-starter-quiz), [quiz table](../content/tables/new-game.json); PR #1).
- **2026-10-31 — Wider tree:** every raisable Rookie gets at least four Champions and every raisable Champion at least two Ultimates; original canonical routes remain, with new links chosen by canon line, family and shared specialty, except canon Otamamon → Geremon ([tree data](../content/tables/evolution-tree.json), [partner rules](reference/partner.md#evolution-rules-for-the-added-species); PRs #1, #26).
- **2026-10-31 — Hunger of large partners:** the hungriest final stages use 8 energy and species with onward evolutions 10; their weight requirements should remain achievable without unreasonable feeding ([species data](../content/species), [care rules](../content/tables/care-rules.json); PR #27).
- **2026-11-01 — Evolution requirements popup:** Square opens a scrolling Needs/You have table over a dimmed chart; requirements should be inspectable rather than guessed, and EVOLUTION GUIDE off hides it ([options: evolution guide](mods/options.md), [partner rules](reference/partner.md#evolution-rules-for-the-added-species); PR #52).
- **2026-11-01 — Care menu priority (superseded by Square quick care):** while its badge shows, care confirm takes priority over nearby machines and NPC interactions; the prompt must perform the action it advertises ([items and menus](reference/items-and-menus.md#using-an-item); PR #69).
- **2026-11-02 — Shared care balance:** the care/reward pass applies to everyone, with no separate balance mode; the revised economy and raising rules are one consistent game ([balance: care and reward pass](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Birth lifetime and Champion wait:** lifetime rises from 360 to 408 hours and Champion → Ultimate wait from 144 to 168; a partner gets another day to train toward its target ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass), [timing table](../content/tables/evolution-rules.json); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Earlier Rookie timing, superseded 2026-11-04:** the care pass changed Rookie wait to 96 hours and Numemon fallback to 120; these were intended to extend raising time but are no longer the current rule ([timing table](../content/tables/evolution-rules.json); PRs #81, #155).
- **2026-11-02 — Lifetime items:** Digiseabass gives +6 hours instead of +3, Chain Melon +24 instead of +20, and Devil Chips cost 6 hours instead of 24; these items should be worth using to stay in a form or gain power ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — File City training:** base gains rise from 8 to 10 (HP/MP 80 to 100), favored-stat multiplier from 1.1 to 1.2, with secondary gains and the 0.9 penalty retained; reduce grind and give training type more identity ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass), [training table](../content/tables/training.json); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Remote gyms:** Leomon and Ice Sanctuary give 15 base points (HP 150) without secondary gains instead of 8/12 with a secondary stat; traveling to them should pay off, while Beetle Land stays at 12 ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass), [gain table](../content/tables/training-gains.json); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Gym tiredness:** every gym session costs 8 tiredness instead of the original 6–10 spread; no stat should be harder to train solely because its exercise is more tiring ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass), [gain table](../content/tables/training-gains.json); PR #87).
- **2026-11-02 — Favored/hated regions:** hourly happiness becomes +3/−3 instead of +1/−1, and tiredness −3/+2 instead of −2/+1; the partner's preferred habitat should matter ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass), [care table](../content/tables/care-rules.json); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Death transformations:** ordinary death-evolution chances rise from 10% to 30%, with Devimon still 50%; players should encounter them without repeated resets ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Evolution items:** item evolution grants the ordinary stat gains, lifetime and tamer-level treatment; rare items should not penalize the player for using them ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Learned-technique requirements:** technique-count bonuses are removed and Gigadramon's bonus becomes 100 battles; a broad move collection should not unexpectedly override the intended stat-based route ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-02 — Monzaemon costume:** Numemon can use it after the toy story too; the original event should not permanently close a later raising route ([balance](mods/balance.md#62-the-care-and-reward-pass); PR #81).
- **2026-11-03 — Starter techniques:** Agumon/Gabumon keep their original techniques, while other quiz starters learn what normal evolution into them teaches, replacing the first-listed move; a new Rookie should not start with an arbitrary powerful technique ([partner: learning](reference/partner.md#learning-techniques); PR #99).
- **2026-11-03 — Snimon/Suijinmon meals:** both eat five times daily instead of three; their energy expenditure assumed five meals and caused prolonged starvation and weight loss ([species records](../content/species); PR #91).
- **2026-11-03 — Hand-made baby families:** Pabumon → Motimon, Pichimon → Bukamon, YukimiBotamon → Nyaromon and Nyokimon → Yokomon add canon baby lines, replacing unsuitable stand-in parents where reachable; each changed Rookie-parent choice is listed with its reason ([baby evolution decisions](mods/hand-made-species.md#8-place-it-in-the-evolution-tree); PR #115).
- **2026-11-03 — Eight rebirth eggs:** NEW DIGIMON on adds the four new Fresh eggs to the original four, including random selection among eight; new families need a rebirth entry point, while off retains only the four original eggs ([partner: rebirth](reference/partner.md#death-and-rebirth); PR #115).
- **2026-11-04 — Mega routes for all Ultimates:** every raisable Ultimate has at least one Mega route, including timed pairs for originals without path rows; raising should not end in an unintended dead end ([tree data](../content/tables/evolution-tree.json), [partner rules](reference/partner.md#evolution-rules-for-the-added-species); PR #136).

## Species & levels

- **By 2026-10-30 — Expanded partners:** native NPCs and 175 imports from Card Battle, World 2 and World 3 become usable partners, with care animations and species records; the roster should extend the original raising game ([species](mods/species.md), [native NPC partners](mods/features/partner-models.md), [imported species](reference/imported-species.md)).
- **2026-10-14 — Names and identity:** use official English one-word names within the 19-character limit, merge duplicate identities and author each species' specialties/moves rather than retaining importer filler; species should read and play as distinct Digimon ([species](mods/species.md), [species records](../content/species)).
- **By 2026-10-30 — Higher stat caps:** offense/defense/speed/brains cap at 9999 and HP/MP/damage at 30000 instead of 999/9999; extended raising and Mega growth need headroom, with old-range combat calculations bounded where necessary ([partner: caps](reference/partner.md#number-limits-and-the-caps-patch), [combat](reference/combat.md)).
- **2026-10-31 — Tier/tree rebuild:** 26 species change Rookie/Champion/Ultimate tier and imported canon Megas use level 6, with tier-appropriate gains, drops, raising data and requirements; the roster should follow its identities and avoid Champion → imported Mega skips ([tree data](../content/tables/evolution-tree.json); PR #26).
- **2026-10-31 — Existing wild difficulty:** 45 placements of tier-moved species keep their former zone stats/bits, including weak Gear Savanna Vermilimon; correcting partner tiers must not silently raise area difficulty ([balance: curated tree](mods/balance.md#jijimons-starter-quiz), [map content](../content/maps); PR #26).
