# challenges

After the ending, a recruited resident can wait at a spot on the island and challenge the partner (docs/mods/resident-challenges.md).
One file per resident whose challenge spot is not its recruit site's file: the challenger's NPC (`alone=` keeps the screen's wild
Digimon away while it waits), its intro, loss and win lines, and its reward. Residents recruited in arcs/residents or elsewhere keep
their challenge in that file. Rows, sites, rewards and pools live in content/tables/resident-challenges.json.
