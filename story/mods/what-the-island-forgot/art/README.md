## Provenance notes

- `dgha01-pass.png`: the notch in the cliff of Overdell's western clearing. Made with Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview) as an edit of a 5x enlargement of the cliff's foot (160x200 pixels of the player's own DGHA01 background at 0,240, with the previous notch in it). The sand pocket was repainted to match the clearing's sand (second iteration of two); only the pocket's pixels (feathered) replace the earlier piece, the rocks are unchanged. The generated image is not kept; the piece is laid over the player's background at build time.
- `mine-head.png`: the Mine Head (screen 337). The entry at the bottom edge, where you arrive from the Old Gallery's drop (315), is a timber-edged flight of steps going down into the cavern, so the arrival reads as a descent. Made with Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview) in three edit requests of the previous art (the first drew steps far too large, the second too faint, the third is the one kept), resized to 896x640; the rest of the cavern was regenerated by the model with the same layout. The six foreground pieces (`foreground/337-*.png`) are cut again from the 2400x1792 original of the kept image with SAM (`maps/author/sam-foreground.py`, prompts in `maps/foreground/mine-head.json`); the large original is a local authoring input (`art/full/`), not kept in the repository.
- `iron-hills-2-toilet.png`, `seam-3-toilet.png`, `descent-2-toilet.png`: the toilet booths of the Iron Hills (318, a rusty sheet-metal booth at the foot of the west slope), the Seam's CAMP 2 (326, the camp's plank latrine against the north wall) and the Overdell Pass (331, a plank latrine against the east rock pillar of the dry riverbed). Each was made with Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview) as one edit of an 8x enlargement of a 160x120 crop of the screen's own art (iron-hills-2.png at 88,114; seam-3.png at 417,53; descent-2.png at 385,288), first iteration each. The booth is the part of the answer that differs from the crop (thin changes such as the removed bush's twigs left out), feathered over our own art and kept with full alpha; `maps/toilets.py` lays it over the imported background with `dwmap.py bg --paste`, so its evening and night colours come from the screen's palettes. The generated images are not kept.
