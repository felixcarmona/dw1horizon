# city

Horizon story sources and authored resources for city.

Resident positions must have a reachable talk contact, including the tamer's
100-unit radius and the native and added NPC footprints. A free collision tile
alone is insufficient: Treasure Hunt's back counter is a disconnected floor
island. Akatorimon stands on the customer floor at (-550, -350); the other added
residents have reachable contacts in the engine movement simulation.
