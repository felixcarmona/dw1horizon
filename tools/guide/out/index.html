Brains does not affect this battle roll. If several techniques pass their rolls, one is learned at random. Repeated casts do not add extra rolls for the same technique.

Chances are per candidate roll, not a guaranteed final award. MP is the base battle cost (data cost ×3), before condition and Brains modifiers. Status percentages apply on a successful hit. Finishers and intrinsic moves have no battle learning chance. Shared names show their data IDs.
