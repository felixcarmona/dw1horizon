; Font b (FONT=b, docs/mods/font.md "Fonts"): our font, drawn in the game's style. tools/fontgen.py font-header b
; writes src/main/font_b.h from it; a character not drawn here falls back to font a (the US letter by pixel).
;
; Format: `glyph CHAR [ADVANCE]` and 11 rows of `#` (ink) and `.`, row 0 first, up to 12 columns. Without ADVANCE the
; ink is moved to column 0 and the advance is its width + 1 (one blank column between letters); with ADVANCE the
; columns stay as drawn (a left bearing, as in a centred digit). Metrics: one baseline, row 8. Capitals, digits and
; ascenders rows 0-8 (9 rows), x-height rows 3-8 (6 rows), descenders rows 9-10. 1-pixel stems. Lowercase accents
; in rows 0-1; capitals carry their accent over a 7-row body (rows 2-8) only when it would not fit, as in extra.txt.
; Every glyph must fit the original glyph's width (its cell, FONT_CELLS): fontgen.py checks it.
; Kerning: `kern LEFTS RIGHTS DELTA`: a glyph of RIGHTS drawn right after one of LEFTS moves DELTA pixels (the first
; line that names a pair decides; a space breaks a pair). The pairs of the approved mockup (media 2026-10-14-165846,
; mock.py): the open capitals T V W Y P F before lowercase and the comma and period, r f y before those two; the
; accented lowercase kern as their letter. fontgen.py refuses a pair whose ink would touch (no blank column left on
; some row), so the mockup's pairs that touched in b are left out: W before lowercase and the period, P before
; m n r u v w x y z ú ü ñ, V Y before ñ, y before the period and the comma.
kern TF aceomnrsuvwxyz.,áàéèóòúüñç -1
kern VY aceomnrsuvwxyz.,áàéèóòúüç -1
kern P aceos.,áàéèóòç -1
kern W , -1
kern rf ., -1
