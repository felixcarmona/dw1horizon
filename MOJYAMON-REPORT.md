# Mojyamon investigation, before implementation

This is the original investigation before the first implementation. Subsequent designs supersede its trade mapping and strict daily sale-value restriction. North Star remains Whamon's item; separate Dolph Fin (unused item 124) now evolves a Rookie into Dolphmon. Eight rows are daily and Small Recovery → Dolph Fin is once per save. See docs/mods/balance.md section 6.1 for current limits and incremental gain ceilings.

Group 1 is the Mojyamon beside Penguinmon on Freezeland screen 134 (script 129). Groups 2 and 3 stand on screen 133 (script 128), in slots 0 and 1. Each group owns three consecutive rows in shops.json. Despite their names, mojyamonGives is what the player hands over and mojyamonTakes is the reward. Nine saved flags record whether each row has ever been completed. The Mojyamon spoken to after all nine are complete joins the city, earns two prosperity and sets its own departure flag. It runs the secret shop in the morning; it does not bring its three exchange rows to that shop.

Always recruiting group 3 is low risk for new recruitment: remove the departure endings from groups 1 and 2 and keep group 3's existing ending. If another group completes the ninth row, the player must return to group 3 and speak to it. Group 3 can still recruit after a cancelled or refused trade because the nine historical completion flags are checked afterwards. Keep existing saves' departure flags and recruitment intact; do not move a previously recruited Mojyamon back out of the city.

North Star, item 92, still has evolution_target 24, Whamon. Dolphmon is species 238. No item in content/items targets 238. North Star does not now lead to Dolphmon. There is no Dolphmon evolution item to give, so skip the gift.

Tapirmon's Market Shed currently sells Digipine for 1000 bits. Hawk Radish has a normal price of 500 bits and is already sold by Pumpkinmon in the Scrap Office. A baby partner pays 90 percent: 900 and 450 respectively. Shops pay 500 for Digipine and 250 for Hawk Radish. None of the proposed exchanges consumes Hawk Radish, so selling it at Tapirmon's cannot feed a profitable trade. Replace only Digipine in its stock.

Black Trout and Digicatfish can be fished at Dragon Eye Lake (screens 6 and 8). There is no direct fishing fee: bait is consumed and rods are required. The fishing table identifies DigiTrout as Black Trout's preferred bait and Moldy Meat as Digicatfish's. Black Trout has no normal shop or ground source in the authored scripts. Digicatfish also appears in Monochromon's rotating daily offer: 2500 bits, with up to two successful haggles lowering it to 2150 and 1900. Black Trout sells for 500 and Digicatfish for 1000.

Red Berry is sold at Tapirmon's for 1000 (900 with a baby), and appears on the ground in Ancient Glacial Region screens 80 and 81. Sweet Nut is sold there at the same price and appears on Gear Savanna screens 70, 71, 74 and 78. Both sell for 500. Ground pickups are once per spot per day and their appearance is random. Both requested inputs are obtainable without changing item sources.

At the configured 50 percent sale rate, the requested output/input sale values are: Happy Mushroom 500 to Ice Mushroom 500; Digiseabass 4000 to Chain Melon 2500; Black Trout 500 to Digipine 500; Digicatfish 1000 to Large MP 1000; Red Berry 500 to Large Recovery 500; Sweet Nut 500 to Medium MP 400; Giant Meat 250 to DigiTrout 150; Medium Recovery 250 to MP Floppy 150. All pass. Digimushroom 50 to Auto Pilot 150 fails even with a daily limit. Use Supercarrot 250 to Auto Pilot 150 instead: it is a food item and already one of the old exchange inputs. Keep all item prices unchanged.

Use nine bits of the existing saved trigger array, one per row, for today's completed trades. Reserve 789 through 797 from the story allocator, beside the vending engine's 798 and 799. The shipped-story lock in this checkout stops at 781. Check other live worktrees before reserving: clean merges can hide numeric collisions. After a rebase, run a full build to check saved-state allocations again. The dailyPStatTrigger routine already clears the vending flags at midnight and on day advances from sleeping/training. Clear these nine flags there too. No new save bytes, day counter, resident array or save format is needed, and loading a save preserves that day's used rows. Retain the nine historical recruitment flags separately. Mark a daily bit only after an exchange succeeds; cancelled or full-bag attempts must not consume it.

The amount box must cap at one and the engine must reject an already-used row before opening it. Reuse the obsolete Yes/No text slot for the refusal line in all eight languages. Build all languages, measure main_BSS_END and MENU, and run the 320-setup heap check plus a same-day rejection and next-day exchange in the emulator.

## Follow-up: a separate Dolphmon item

Item 124, AS Decoder, has no story give/take/check, shop stock or species drop in this branch or current main (8b15663c). It is separate from the real Frig Key, item 123, which Myotismon gives and the mansion refrigerator consumes. Reusing 124 is clean; extending past 127 would need wider assumptions in the icon grid, shop stock and seven-bit trade-menu item encoding.

The custom evolution-item table already reserves four rows, but only Giga Hand, Noble Mane and Metalbanana use them. Dolph Fin occupies the fourth row: item 124, Dolphmon 238, required partner level 3 (Rookie). The handler and refusal bounds include 124, and unavailable imports are rejected by the shared rule lookup. Names and descriptions replace existing slot 124 in all eight languages. The icon reuses the unused AS Decoder sprite with an existing blue palette. No table, atlas or save field grows. The investigated main snapshot (8b15663c) had no Chinese text table. After Chinese integration, the item and trade-limit text also have Simplified Chinese translations; the current names and limits cover all nine languages.

North Star stays item 92 and targets Whamon 24. Whamon's current Ultimate level requires a Champion; NEW DIGIMON off restores Whamon's Champion level and Rookie eligibility. The item-stage helper keeps the established native Mega-item exception while following Whamon's current stage.

Tapirmon's generic shop has global prices: both its menu and charge read ITEM_PARA, and shop(...) exposes stock only. Vegiemon's separate scripted purchases already specify 500 directly. A Tapirmon-only price would need a new override or a replacement menu, so the conditional 700-bit change is skipped. No global item value or Vegiemon price changes.

The additional daily gain over selling owned inputs is 200 if all eight rows are used, or 1800 if only positive sale-value rows are chosen. The latter is 300 after the 1500-bit Birdramon trip. Buying only profitable purchasable inputs yields 999 normally or 1109 with baby discounts, before travel. Digiseabass is not sold; mixed purchase-and-free-fishing cash totals are separate from opportunity-cost gains. Dolph Fin's 2450 gain over selling Small Recovery is once per save, excluded from every daily ceiling. Balance section 6.1 gives all scenarios.
